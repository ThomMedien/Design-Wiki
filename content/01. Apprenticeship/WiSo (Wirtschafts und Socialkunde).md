# Wirtschafts- und Sozialkunde (WiSo)

The WiSo written exam covers basic economics, employment law, the German social security system, and business organization. It is a critical part of your overall exam score.

---

## 1. The Training Contract (Der Ausbildungsvertrag)

The legal foundation of your apprenticeship is the *Berufsausbildungsvertrag (BBiG)*. It must be written down before the training begins.

### Key Regulations:
*   **Probezeit (Probation Period):** Must be between **1 and 4 months**. During this period, either side can terminate the contract immediately without giving reasons and without a notice period.
*   **Termination after Probation:**
    *   **By the Apprentice:** 4 weeks notice if switching to another career path or stopping the apprenticeship.
    *   **By the Employer:** Only possible for a serious cause (*wichtiger Grund*, e.g., theft or continuous refusal of duties) without notice. Prior warnings (*Abmahnungen*) are usually required.
*   **Duties of the Apprentice (Pflichten des Auszubildenden):**
    *   *Lernpflicht:* Endeavor to acquire the skills.
    *   *Sorgfaltspflicht:* Handle tools and equipment with care.
    *   *Schweigepflicht:* Maintain operational secrecy.
    *   *Berichtsheftführung:* Keep a regular training record (required to take the exams).
*   **Duties of the Employer (Pflichten des Ausbildenden):**
    *   *Ausbildungspflicht:* Teach the training contents.
    *   *Freistellungspflicht:* Release the apprentice for school and exams.
    *   *Vergütungspflicht:* Pay a fair, rising monthly allowance.
    *   *Fürsorgepflicht:* Ensure safety and health at the workplace.

## 2. The Five Pillars of German Social Insurance (Sozialversicherungen)

Social insurance is compulsory. Premiums are generally split 50/50 between the employer and employee (except for Accident Insurance).

| Insurance (Versicherung) | Responsibility | Triggers / Focus | Funding |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Krankenversicherung** (Health) | Krankenkassen (AOK, TK, etc.) | Medical treatment, sick pay (*Krankengeld*). | Split (Employer & Employee) |
| **Rentenversicherung** (Pension) | Deutsche Rentenversicherung | Financial security in old age, disability. | Split (Employer & Employee) |
| **Arbeitslosenversicherung** (Unemployment) | Bundesagentur für Arbeit | Unemployment benefits (*ALG I*), job placement. | Split (Employer & Employee) |
| **Pflegeversicherung** (Long-term care) | Pflegekassen (tied to Health) | Care costs due to age or severe illness/injury. | Split (Employer & Employee) |
| **Unfallversicherung** (Accident) | Berufsgenossenschaften (BG) | Work accidents, commuting accidents, occupational diseases. | **100% Employer** |

## 3. Youth Labor Protection Act (Jugendarbeitsschutzgesetz - JArbSchG)

Applies to apprentices **under 18 years of age**. 

*   **Working Hours:** Maximum **8 hours per day** and **40 hours per week**. Can be extended to 8.5 hours if compensated on other days.
*   **Rest Breaks (Ruhepausen):**
    *   **30 minutes** for working times between 4.5 and 6 hours.
    *   **60 minutes** for working times over 6 hours.
    *   Breaks must last at least 15 minutes each to count.
*   **Work-free Days:** Generally a 5-day week. Saturdays and Sundays are free (exceptions exist for specific service industries, but compensatory time off must be granted).
*   **Min. Annual Vacation Days (Urlaub):**
    *   Under 16 at the start of calendar year: **30 working days**.
    *   Under 17 at the start of calendar year: **27 working days**.
    *   Under 18 at the start of calendar year: **25 working days**.

## 4. Co-determination (Betriebliche Mitbestimmung)

### Works Council (Betriebsrat)
*   Can be formed in companies with at least **5 permanent employees** who are eligible to vote.
*   Represents employee interests regarding work conditions, health, and terminations.

### Youth & Apprentice Representation (JAV)
*   Formed in companies with at least **5 employees under 18** or **apprentices under 25**, *if* a Betriebsrat already exists.
*   Represents the specific interests of trainees to the Betriebsrat.

## 5. Capacity to Contract (Geschäftsfähigkeit)

| Stage | Age | Legal Status | Validity of Contracts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Geschäftsunfähig** | 0 to 6 years | No capacity | Contracts are always **nichtig** (void). |
| **Beschränkt geschäftsfähig** | 7 to 17 years | Limited capacity | Requires parent/guardian consent. Otherwise **schwebend unwirksam** (pending). Exception: **Taschengeldparagraf** (§ 110 BGB). |
| **Voll geschäftsfähig** | 18+ years | Full capacity | Fully valid contracts. |
