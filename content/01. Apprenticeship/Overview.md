# Mediengestaltung Digital und Print: Ausbildung Guide

This guide covers the essential elements of the German vocational training (Ausbildung) for **Mediengestalter Digital und Print**, updated according to the **2023 restructuring**.

## Part 1: The Four Specializations (Fachrichtungen)

As of August 2023, the training is divided into four distinct paths. While the first two years share a common foundation, you will specialize in the third year.

### 1. Digitalmedien (Digital Media)
*   **Focus:** Technical implementation of digital products.
*   **Key Skills:** Web development (HTML/CSS/JS), UX/UI design, app prototyping, and audio/video data preparation.

### 2. Printmedien (Print Media)
*   **Focus:** Technical preparation for physical print.
*   **Key Skills:** High-end image retouching, color management (ICC profiles), PDF standards (PDF/X), and print production workflows.

### 3. Designkonzeption (Design Conception)
*   **Focus:** Developing the "Idea."
*   **Key Skills:** Visual storytelling, creating design systems, presenting concepts to clients, and creative research.

### 4. Projektmanagement (Project Management)
*   **Focus:** The business side of media.
*   **Key Skills:** Calculating costs (Kalkulation), customer consulting, project planning, and legal frameworks.

## Part 2: Core Elements You Must Master

Regardless of your specialization, these "Basisqualifikationen" are the bread and butter of the exams.

### 1. Design Fundamentals (Gestaltungsgrundlagen)
*   **Typography:** Typeface categories, micro-typography (kerning, tracking), and hierarchy.
*   **Color Theory:** Additive (RGB) vs. Subtractive (CMYK) mixing, color harmony, and psychological effects of color.
*   **Composition:** Grid systems, the Golden Ratio, and whitespace management.

### 2. Software Proficiency (Adobe Creative Cloud)
*   **InDesign:** Layout, master pages, and print styles.
*   **Photoshop:** Non-destructive editing, masking, and color correction.
*   **Illustrator:** Vector paths, logos, and icon design.

### 3. Technical Knowledge
*   **File Formats:** Knowing when to use JPEG, PNG, SVG, TIFF, or EPS.
*   **Resolution:** Difference between DPI (print) and PPI (digital).
*   **Media Law:** Copyright (Urheberrecht), GDPR (DSGVO), and licensing.

## Part 3: The Exams (The "Gestreckte Abschlussprüfung")

The final grade is split into two parts. You don't just have one big exam at the end anymore.

### Part 1 (AP1): After ~18 Months
*   **Weight:** Counts for **25%** of your final grade.
*   **Content:** Basic design and technical tasks (usually a mix of a practical project and written questions).

### Part 2 (AP2): At the end of Year 3
*   **Weight:** Counts for **75%** of your final grade.
*   **Components:**
    *   **Practical Project (Prüfungsstück):** You are given a briefing (e.g., "Design a brand for a cafe") and must produce a solution within a set timeframe (usually 20-30 hours) including documentation.
    *   **Presentation/Colloquium:** You present your project to the IHK board and answer technical questions.
    *   **Written Exams:** Three main papers:
        *   **Communication & Design** (Concept/Layout)
        *   **Media Production** (Technical implementation)
        *   **WISO** (Economic and Social Studies - labor law, insurance, etc.)

## Part 4: Pro-Tips for Passing
*   **Learn the "IHK-Way":** The exam board looks for specific technical terms. Use official terminology (e.g., use "Satzspiegel" instead of just "margin").
*   **Documentation is Key:** In the practical exam, your process and rationale (why you chose a certain font or color) are often worth more points than the final design itself.
*   **Stay Updated:** Follow **ZFA Medien** (the central committee). They publish sample exams and the "Prüfungskatalog" which lists every possible topic that can be tested.
